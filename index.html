Questions people actually ask

What does “AI-assisted” actually mean?

We use AI to draft code, copy and research fast. Plans, decisions and quality control are human. You see the reasoning and the sources — no black boxes.

How much does a project cost?

Starting points: audit from $150, fix sprint from $750, build from $1,500, care plan from $100/month. Everything gets a written scope and fixed quote before money moves.

How fast can you start?

Reply within one business day. Small work scoped in 2-3 days. Most audits, fix sprints and landing pages ship inside 1-2 weeks.

Who owns the code?

You do, from the first commit. Delivered in a repo you control, private or public. Our own tools stay Apache-2.0 open source.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. NDAs and private repos are fine. We default to public work because it makes our proof verifiable — never required.

What if I am not happy?

Cancel before the first paid week starts for a full refund of the upfront half. After that, you can stop at week boundaries for the price of weeks used.

One specific message beats ten perfect ones. Include the goal, budget range and date if you have them — we reply within one business day with scoped options.
