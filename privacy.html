Privacy

Effective 26 October 2026. Short version: this site has no analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels. We only learn about you when you contact us.

What we collect

The enquiry form sends your email and message (plus hidden routing fields) to FormSubmit, which forwards it to meetcode99@gmail.com. FormSubmit's own handling is described on their site. We do not run any other form or service.

Logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's standard web logs may record requests. We do not access or process those logs.

Fonts

Typography is loaded from Google Fonts; their servers see the request. Nothing else from your browser is collected by us.

Client engagements

Email, documents and code shared during an engagement stay with the engagement. NDAs are honored. Nothing is published about client work without written permission.

Deletion

Email meetcode99@gmail.com asking to delete correspondence and we will (subject to legal retention duties, which is a fancy way of saying: we are a small studio, not a data broker).
